RHS Senior Science Team

The Science Team is for students who are interested in attending science challenges where we will compete with other schools. Students will participate in events that test their various scientific knowledge that they have learned in class. The club will have weekly meetings leading up to competition day in order to make sure all members are prepared. New members are always welcome to attend meetings even if they don't plan to attend competition day.

I compete for my school Senior Science Team on the Pre-Build 2: Pole Climber for the 48th UBC Physics Olympics 2026

Here are the rules:
Build a pole climber, which will be able to carry the team's iPhone up and down a PVC pole. The pole will have an outer diameter of 33.8 mm (PVC 1', 25mm inner diameter) and length 1.50 m. The climber cannot damage the pole in any way. The climbing speed on the way up and down must be as constant as possible and cannot exceed 0.5 m/s. The time of the whole climb up and down cannot exceed 1 minute. The climb will be monitored by a Phyphox app on the iPhone, which must record the absolute acceleration of the climber (without g) every 0.01s. The app is available for free in the App Store. The score is given by the formula: aav = Σiai/n
where Σi means that we sum all the values of acceleration measured on the way up and down and n is the number of data points. The smallest aav wins.

A recognition of achievement

Story Time

I am the main builder and programmer for the robot, and my initial plan was to use Arduino as the main processor of the robot. After a long time of testing, we realise that the motor does not have enough torque to lift itself up, the power supply does not have enough current, and the sensor is very slow. Our team was about to give up. However, when I returned home, I suddenly remembered my grandmother gave me a box of EV3 set many years ago, and those LEGO EV3 motors have a pretty good amount of torque. Therefore, I rebuilt the entire prototype in one night.


Outcome

My team achieve rank 6 with the Pre-Build 2: Pole Climber for my school, Richmond Secondary School
